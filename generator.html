    Scan tips
    • Keep strong contrast: dark code on a light background works best.
    • Don't shrink the quiet zone below 4 modules for print.
    • Always scan the final file (and the printed copy) with a phone before sharing.
    • Static codes can't be edited after printing.

    About static QR codes

    The codes made here are static: your content is written directly into the pattern, so they work forever, need no account and track nothing. The flip side is that the content can't be changed after you print. See the Help & FAQ for details.
